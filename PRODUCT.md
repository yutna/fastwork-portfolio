# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Jekyll 4 on GitHub Pages, with plain HTML, CSS, and ESM JavaScript.

## Users

People arriving from Yutthana's Fastwork profile: Thai and international SMB owners, founders, and teams evaluating a freelance software craftsman. They have already seen the profile bio and the Krua Tumya project post, and they often skim on mobile.

## Product Purpose

A one-project portfolio that shows how Yutthana builds for the web and gives the visitor a clear next step. Success means the visitor opens the live Krua Tumya site or starts a conversation on Fastwork.

## Positioning

Yutthana practises software craftsmanship: complex web apps, performance, accessibility, and maintainable code. The portfolio proves this through one project, a short capability index, and working notes — not through design-role language.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from <https://fastwork.com/user/yutna>, where the Krua Tumya post already shows the project screenshots and a description. The portfolio links back to Fastwork and GitHub. The demo is self-initiated, so there are no client testimonials, ratings, or outcome metrics to show.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One project: Krua Tumya (ครัวตำย่า).
- English body copy.
- Jekyll with `baseurl: /fastwork-portfolio`.
- No fake projects, testimonials, metrics, or clients.
- No analytics, trackers, cookies, or third-party runtime dependencies.
- No green in the UI.
- Keep the Krua Tumya demo page unchanged.
- WCAG A/AA, visible keyboard focus, reduced motion, and no horizontal overflow at 390, 768, and 1440 pixels.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Yutthana Siphuengchai. Location: Chiang Mai, Thailand. Voice: direct, specific, technical, and free of hype. English. Fastwork is the primary contact path. The homepage is monochrome, uses Schibsted Grotesk, and avoids design-role language. No face mark, no green.

## Evidence on Hand

- Live demo: `/krua-tumya/` — a restaurant website with a heat meter, a plate builder, and live pricing.
- Fastwork profile: <https://fastwork.com/user/yutna>.
- GitHub: <https://github.com/yutna>.
- No testimonials, ratings, client logos, or outcome metrics exist. Do not invent them.

## Product Principles

1. Show the work. One project, shown properly, beats a thin index.
2. Use engineering language. Describe what was built, not a design persona.
3. Make performance and accessibility part of the offer, not decoration.
4. Keep the interface quiet so the work and the writing carry the page.
5. Make the next step obvious: open the live site or start a conversation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA, visible keyboard focus, reduced motion, responsive layouts at 390, 768, and 1440 pixels. Thai text inside project screenshots is part of the work and must not be edited.
